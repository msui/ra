Relationship Anarchy Smorgasbord

This is a self-contained quiz that helps you and your partner(s) privately think through what you each want out of your relationship — things like emotional intimacy, physical affection, shared finances, or long-term commitment — without assuming a default relationship "package." Everything runs locally in your browser; nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Download the quiz

The download is a single HTML file. Open it in any browser (double-click it, or drag it into a browser window) — no install or internet connection required.

Next steps

  1. Download the file and open it in your browser.
  2. Go through the quiz privately and rate each item.
  3. Export your results and share the file with your partner(s) however you'd like (text, AirDrop, email).
  4. One of you imports everyone's results files into the same app to build a shared results page for discussion.

Relationship Smorgasbord Quiz

This multiple-choice quiz helps you and your partner(s) think through what you each want out of your relationship — things like emotional intimacy, physical affection, shared finances, or long-term commitment — without assuming a default relationship "package."

Two ways to take the quiz:

📱 Online Quiz ⬇️ Download for Offline Use

Online: Open and complete the quiz right here in your browser, ready to use now.
Offline: Download a single HTML file to your device. No internet connection needed after downloading, and everything happens on your device (unless you choose to upload and share your results with someone).

Quick start guide

  1. Choose "Online Quiz" to start now in your browser, or "Download for Offline Use" to download and open locally.
  2. Go through the quiz and rate each item.
  3. Export your results and send your answer file to your partner(s) however you'd like (text, AirDrop, email).
  4. One of you imports everyone's results files to their device to build a shared results page for discussion. (Or all of you can do this step individually, if you each want a copy of the combined results on your device.)

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between the Online and Offline quiz?

Online: Take the quiz right now in your browser over your current internet connection. Results are stored in your browser until you export them.
Offline: Download the quiz to your device and complete it without an internet connection. Everything stays on your device with no data transmitted online with privacy in mind (unless you choose to upload and share your results with a partner).

How do I open the quiz on a phone? (Offline version)

When using the offline version on a phone or tablet, how you open the file after downloading affects whether your progress reliably saves. It works best to open it from your Files/Downloads app rather than straight from a browser preview.

iPhone

  1. Tap the "Download for Offline Use" button — Safari saves it to your Files app (usually in Downloads).
  2. Open the Files app and find it. Tapping it once may only open a "Quick Look" preview — don't rate the quiz from there. Tap the share icon and choose "Open in Safari" instead.
  3. Fill it out from there.

Android

  1. Tap the "Download for Offline Use" button — it'll download to your Downloads folder.
  2. Open it from Downloads (or the download notification) so it opens in the full Chrome app — not a "lite" in-app browser (like the one inside Gmail, Messages, or social apps), which won't reliably save your progress.
  3. Fill it out from there.

Doing it this way makes auto-saving quiz progress far more reliable. And if you get interrupted partway, you can hit Save My Progress inside the quiz to download a small file to resume from later.

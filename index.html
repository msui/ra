Relationship Anarchy Smorgasbord

This is a self-contained quiz that helps you and your partner(s) privately think through what you each want out of your relationship — things like emotional intimacy, physical affection, shared finances, or long-term commitment — without assuming a default relationship "package." Everything runs locally in your browser; nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Download the quiz (html file)
  • Look for a file named something like relationship-anarchy-smorgasbord.html in your device's Downloads folder.
  • The download is a single HTML file. Open it in any browser (double-click it, or drag it into a browser window) — no install or internet connection required.

Next steps

  1. Download the file and open it in your browser.
  2. Go through the quiz privately and rate each item.
  3. Export your results and share the file with your partner(s) however you'd like (text, AirDrop, email).
  4. One of you imports everyone's results files into the same app to build a shared results page for discussion.

Opening it on your phone

How you open the file after downloading affects whether your progress reliably saves. On mobile, it works best to open it from your Files/Downloads app rather than straight from a browser preview.

iPhone

  1. Tap the download button above — Safari saves it to your Files app (usually in Downloads).
  2. Open the Files app, find it, tap once, then tap the share icon and choose "Open in Safari."
  3. Fill it out from there.

Android

  1. Tap the download button above — it'll download to your Downloads folder.
  2. Open it from Downloads (or the download notification) — it opens in Chrome.
  3. Fill it out from there.

Doing it this way makes auto-saving quiz progress far more reliable. And if you get interrupted partway, you can hit Save My Progress inside the quiz to download a small file to resume from later.
